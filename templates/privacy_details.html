Website privacy and cookies

Updated 27 November 2026.

BeHeard Zimbabwe is based at 2539 Chinotimba, Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe. For privacy questions, contact info@beheardzim.org or +263 72 011 6342.

Information used by this website

You can browse the public website without creating an account. The donation popup uses your selected amount and frequency within the page to display transfer instructions. These selections are not submitted to BeHeard by the popup.

When you contact us by email, WhatsApp or telephone, you choose what information to share. Your contact details and message are used to respond to your enquiry and coordinate the support or donation you request. Avoid sending sensitive counselling information, identity documents, passwords, card numbers or payment PINs through website donation enquiries.

Website forms

General enquiries, volunteer applications and counselling appointment requests are submitted to the private Flask service over HTTPS. Name, email, optional phone, the submitted message and a timestamped consent record are stored in private editor snapshots on Supabase. A protected founder inbox displays them. A hashed request fingerprint is retained with the submission for abuse prevention. Counselling requests ask for appointment preferences and contact details only. Consent is required before submitting. Email notifications contain a reference, with details available after founder sign-in. Notifications remain pending until the sending service is configured.

Donations and external services

Bank and EcoCash transfers are completed through your bank or wallet service. Mukuru, WorldRemit and similar transfer enquiries open WhatsApp to request receiving instructions. The website does not process card payments or verify that a transfer has completed. Payment and messaging services handle information under their own privacy terms.

Social-media, email and WhatsApp links take you to external services. Their privacy settings and policies apply when you use them. Clicking a link can share technical information with that service.

Cookies and browser storage

The public website does not currently include advertising or audience-tracking scripts. It stores your selected colour mode in your browser’s local storage under the key beheard-theme. You can remove this preference by clearing this site’s browser data.

The private founder dashboard uses a secure session cookie for authentication and protection of editing requests. Hosting services may process technical request information, including IP addresses, browser information and requested pages, to deliver the website and maintain security.

If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, this notice and the relevant choices will be updated before they are enabled. Optional tracking will require an informed choice with an equally accessible way to decline or withdraw it.

Hosting, access and retention

Render delivers the website and hosts the founder dashboard. Supabase stores private editor snapshots, including drafts and account state. These services may process data outside Zimbabwe. Founder access is password protected, and public exports exclude private drafts, account records and server credentials.

Enquiry records and donation correspondence should be retained only as needed to respond, administer the requested activity and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact us to ask about a specific record or request access, correction or deletion. Some records may need to be retained where a legal obligation applies.

Children, photographs and stories

Beneficiary photographs and personal stories must only be published with appropriate consent and safeguarding measures. Contact us if you are concerned about a photograph or story, or wish to request its removal.
